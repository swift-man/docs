LaunchingView

@Composable
fun LaunchingView(content: @Composable () -> Unit, launchScreen: @Composable () -> Unit, isFinished: Boolean = true, service: LaunchingServiceClient, defaultText: LaunchingAlertDefaultText = LaunchingAlertDefaultText(), onOpenUri: (Uri) -> Unit = rememberDefaultUriOpener())(source)

Launch gate that keeps launchScreen visible until the launch policy is known, then shows content.

Behavior mirrors the iOS LaunchingView:

  • The policy is fetched through service on the first display and on every foreground return. Re-creating a foreground Activity (rotation, in-app theme change) restores the last policy and the dialogs that were open without fetching again, unless a fetch was running or had failed. An Activity re-created after being stopped, destroyed in the background, or after process death restores the policy and fetches again.

  • Valid shows content once isFinished is also true; otherwise launchScreen stays.

  • OptionalUpdateRequired shows content under a cancel/update dialog.

  • ForcedUpdateRequired and terminating Notice replace everything with the blocking launch screen.

  • A non-terminating Notice shows content under a notice dialog whose button opens doneUri.

  • A thrown fetch shows an error dialog; dismissing it fetches again.

Parameters

content

The app content shown when the policy allows it.

launchScreen

The screen shown while the policy is unknown or isFinished is false.

isFinished

Set to false to keep launchScreen until the app's own startup work is done.

service

Supplies AppUpdateStatus; pass a stable instance.

defaultText

Text used when Remote Config titles or messages are empty.

onOpenUri

Opens update and notice links; defaults to an ACTION_VIEW intent.
